About

Joe Sims is a Slidell-based singer-songwriter whose music is a soulful reflection of over 20 years in the New Orleans music scene. A registered nurse by trade and a musician by heart, Joe weaves his unique life experiences into a "musical gumbo" of blues, rock, and heartfelt Americana.

His authentic storytelling and seasoned performance style have earned him coveted spots sharing the stage with some of the finest names in the industry. Joe has opened for a diverse roster of acclaimed artists, including The Band of Heathens, Shinyribs, Chuck Cannon, Andrew Duhon, and Country hitmaker Deana Carter. Whether performing in intimate songwriter rounds or at festivals, Joe continues to deliver a sound that is deeply rooted in the grit and melody of South Louisiana.

Booking Configurations: Available as Solo Acoustic Songwriter, Acoustic Duo, or Intimate Songwriter Round.
